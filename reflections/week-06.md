# Week 6 — Key Takeaways

- PKI issues follow a structured troubleshooting process: retrieve, parse, validate chain, check trust
- Expired certificates fail due to invalid dates
- Broken chains fail because of missing intermediate certificates
- SAN mismatch fails because hostname does not match certificate identity
- Wildcard certificates only cover one level of subdomains
- TLS failures can look similar but have different root causes
